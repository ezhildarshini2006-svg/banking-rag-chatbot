Savings Account
A savings account allows customers to deposit money and earn interest. Customers can withdraw money using supported banking services.

Personal Loan
A personal loan is an unsecured loan that can be used for personal expenses. Interest rates and eligibility depend on the bank's policies.

Credit Card
A credit card allows customers to make purchases using a predefined credit limit. Customers must make payments according to their billing statement.

Debit Card
A debit card allows customers to make purchases and withdraw cash directly from their linked bank account.

Fund Transfer
Customers can transfer money between bank accounts using supported banking transfer services.

Fixed Deposit
A fixed deposit allows customers to deposit money for a specific period and earn interest according to the applicable rate.

EMI
EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the fixed amount paid periodically toward a loan.
